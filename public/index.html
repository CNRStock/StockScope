NO BLACK BOX

How the maths works.

The point is to make the calculation inspectable, not magical.

01

Schedule contributions

Generate daily, weekly or monthly cash contributions from the user's selected dates.

02

Resolve trading days

If a contribution lands on a weekend or market holiday, cash is queued and invested on the next available trading day.

03

Use adjusted prices

Fractional shares are purchased using adjusted daily prices so historical stock splits don't distort the result.

04

Track every purchase

The engine accumulates fractional shares and cumulative cash invested for the entire period.

05

Value the portfolio

At each trading date, all accumulated shares are valued at that day's adjusted close to create the chart.

06

Separate facts from AI

Market maths and similarity ranking stay deterministic. AI explains sourced facts; it does not fabricate the score.
